2. WHAT ROUTEWIZE IS
-------------------------------------------------------------
routewize is an API proxy that sits between your application and
your own OpenAI and/or Anthropic accounts. It provides:

  (a) Semantic response caching — detecting near-duplicate requests
      and serving a prior response instead of making a new,
      billable call to a Provider.
  (b) Cost-based model routing — routing requests to a less
      expensive model according to rules you configure, never by
      automatic judgment of answer quality.
  (c) Provider-native prompt caching, where supported by the
      underlying Provider.
  (d) Usage and savings reporting, based on request metadata
      routewize logs about your traffic (see the Privacy Policy for
      exactly what is and is not recorded).

routewize does not replace your relationship with OpenAI or
Anthropic (each, a "Provider"). You must hold your own valid
account and API credentials with each Provider you configure.
routewize never resells, marks up, or otherwise touches your
Provider billing relationship — you are billed by the Provider
directly for whatever usage routewize's routing and caching
decisions produce.
